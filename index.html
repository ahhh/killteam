About this simulator

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan-made simulation project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or approved by Games Workshop. Warhammer 40,000, Kill Team, faction names, and related marks and game materials are the property of their respective owners. The project uses original interface artwork and machine-generated operative portraits, and does not reproduce official miniature photography or illustrations. Rules references and compatibility information are provided for identification and educational/simulation purposes only. Where official rules data is required, users should consult the current official Games Workshop sources.

What is bundled

Original code, an original interface, original geometric battlefield tokens and terrain, machine-generated operative portraits and the head tokens cropped from them, and synthetic demo teams invented for this engine. No official stat lines, weapon profiles, ploys, or equipment are included.

Simulating a real team

Load a rule pack you have sourced yourself through Teams… → Load your own rule pack. Imported packs stay in your browser. Check the current official downloads for the live rules: official Kill Team downloads.

Determinism

Every die roll comes from one seeded generator. The same seed, map, teams, engine version, and AI version always produce an identical battle, so any result can be reproduced and audited.
